What it will look like

Reading the drawings

On a phone, tap the drawing title to open its description and the Legend button for the colors. Members drawn in gold are concepts not yet on any sheet; orange marks items pending the engineer of record or drawn from interpretation rather than a dimension. Faded gray is house context.
  • Layers — the checkboxes along the bottom hide or show framing, footings, finishes, labels and dimensions.
  • Views — the buttons jump the camera to a preset; drag anywhere to orbit from there.
  • Links — add ?view=, &show= or &hide= to a drawing's address to open it on a specific view and layer set.
Not engineered — do not build from these. The drawings interpret the Armstrong Design permit set (project 2507) and the Pieruccioni E&C structural sheets together with field conditions the owner has recorded; every dimension is traced to a sheet or a note in the project wiki. Field changes are shown as reported and flagged where nothing is on record.
