What it will look like

Reading the drawings

Every drawing opens clean. Tick Caption under Show to read its description (on a phone, tap the title to expand it), Labels and Dimensions for the call-outs, and the Legend button on a phone for the colors; the All drawings link in the top-left corner comes back to this page. Members drawn in gold are concepts not yet on any sheet; orange marks items pending the engineer of record or drawn from interpretation rather than a dimension. Faded gray is house context.
  • Layers — the checkboxes along the bottom hide or show framing, footings, finishes, labels, dimensions and the caption. Labels, dimensions and the caption start off so the geometry reads clean.
  • Views — the buttons jump the camera to a preset; drag anywhere to orbit from there.
  • Walk — the arrow keys or WASD move you through the model, PageUp/PageDown change eye height, Shift runs; the mouse keeps orbiting. Click the drawing first so it has keyboard focus.
  • Links — the small buttons under each card open a drawing already set to a particular view or step, so a shared link shows exactly what the sender was looking at.
Not engineered — do not build from these. The drawings interpret the Armstrong Design permit set (project 2507) and the Pieruccioni E&C structural sheets together with field conditions the owner has recorded; every dimension is traced to a sheet or a note in the project wiki. Field changes are shown as reported and flagged where nothing is on record.
