House exterior

The whole house in three siding colours -- Greenblack, black and Iron Ore -- from six views, each with black trim.
The corner posts and the roof beams they carry are boxed to look like timbers, in a darker brown stain (a stand-in; the stain product is not chosen). A soft light is added under the covered decks only; walls in the open keep their true darkness. Colours are the Sherwin-Williams published values, Greenblack SW 6994 and Iron Ore SW 7069, shown at chip-true brightness; the black is the reference build's black. None of the three is a chosen colour yet. The sun is placed per view so the side with the door or the most windows reads clearly; it is not a real time of day. The horizon is made from the owner's photographs of the lot taken the morning of 2026-09-24; the grass is a stand-in.
