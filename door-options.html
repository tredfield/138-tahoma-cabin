Parts: sills, weatherstrip and hinges

All-black sill

Sill = the strip on the floor under the door. Here both the raised strip and the metal plate you step on are black or near-black.

"Black cap" sill

Only the raised strip is black. The metal you step on stays silver (or bronze). This is what Masonite uses.

Bronze sill (the Codel on your quote)

Codel offers mill (silver) or bronze. Bronze is dark brown and can look almost black in photos, so look at a real one.

Weatherstrip colours

Weatherstrip = the foam strip in the frame that the door closes against. Codel's is brown, or white on request.

Hinges: black vs oil-rubbed bronze

Black hinges, with oil-rubbed bronze for comparison: it is also dark but brown with copper edges.
