Kitchen & great room

Alder or hickory cabinets, the island matching or painted Evergreen, light quartz or black soapstone counters, on cream walls.
KraftMaid Thornton five-piece doors in the colour of the owner's sample doors (Barley stain), grain from a plank photograph. Counters are light quartz, or the Home Depot Black Soapstone the owner's wife chose, built from the vendor's slab photographs. The kitchen faucet is the Moen 7260BL Belfield the owner's wife selected. Rebuilt after the owner's 2026-09-16 review with six drawing corrections: the upper cabinets now stop clear of the window casing, base trim is 4" and runs the living room, the stair-foot post sits flush in the loft-edge corner, and the gable windows lost their stool and apron while the triangles lost their mitre spikes. Colours are approximate until KraftMaid's finish images and the paint chip are in hand; quartz, tile and floor are look-alikes. Nothing is staged; the stools and table are placeholders, and the window views are stand-ins.
