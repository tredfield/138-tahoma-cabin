Kitchen & great room

The lower-floor kitchen with the owner's own KraftMaid Thornton sample doors on the cabinets: rustic alder or hickory on the perimeter, and the island either matching or painted Evergreen, on cream walls. Six views and six sheets comparing the options.
The cabinet wood is the owner's sample doors photographed under kitchen light, so the grain and scale are real and the hue is approximate until KraftMaid's finish images replace it; the island green is the sample's colour under the same light. The walls are a cream read from a reference photograph, paint chip pending. One 14 1/2" sample door is the whole source of each wood, so its knots recur every 13.7" up a tall door. Quartz, tile and floor are still look-alikes. Nothing is staged; the stools and dining table are measured placeholders. The north view is the owner's Rainier photograph stretched wide; every other window shows a stock sky.
