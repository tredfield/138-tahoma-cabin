Deck beams and posts

  • The roof beams over both decks run into the gable walls. There's one at each corner (6x8 at the front, 6x10 at the rear) and a ridge beam high in the middle of each gable wall. Put the cedar boxes on those beams before siding, so the siding is cut to the finished box. Each box adds about 3/4" to 1" per side.
  • The boxes are rough-sawn cedar with mitred corners, on the corner posts above the deck floor and on the 6x8 and 6x10 beams. Each beam box wraps over its post top, and the beam ends get an end board. The stain colour comes later. Approximate sizes, to confirm with the finish carpenter: posts about 7" square, porch beams about 7" x 8 3/4", rear beams about 7" x 10 3/4".
  • At each end of both ground-level decks, the end board and cedar skirt run back to the wall. Set them before the bottom row of siding.

Every wall

  • Window and door flashing fixes from the Nov 3 walk:
    • tape pressed down firmly
    • sill tape turned up the sides, with the corners closed
    • head tape at the triangle windows tucked under the house wrap
    • head tape on the one lower window that's missing it
    • sill pans at every window and door
    • a photo of each opening before it's covered
  • Bump-out walls over the lower roof (east, and check the west one): house wrap lapped over the metal roof-to-wall flashing, not under it, and a kick-out flashing at the bottom of each run
  • Every outlet, light, vent cap and hose bib gets a mounting block so its cover sits flat on the siding. Outdoor outlets are GFCI with in-use (bubble) covers.
  • Siding bottom edge clear of the ground (the plans say wood at least 8" above soil), with a gap above deck boards and lower roofs per Hardie's install rules
  • Keep the bottom of the siding clear of the foundation flood vents
  • Z-flashing at horizontal panel joints (the two-story gables), and drip cap over the water table and the window and door head trim
  • Along the east and west walls, the soffit boards go up before the top row of siding, because the siding closes the step between the two eave levels
